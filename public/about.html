About

Why we started.

A small, quiet practice built around objects that are honestly described, made to last, and priced without theatre.

Astro Vetro began with a simple frustration: most of what is sold as spiritual is loud, and most of what is genuinely useful is not sold at all. We wanted the opposite of that.

So we kept the range small and the descriptions plain. If a stone has inclusions, we say so. If a piece is a gift for someone other than the person who ordered it, we say that too. The aim is a collection you can buy once and keep for years, rather than a rotation of objects that need replacing.

How we choose

Everything is picked in person, in small batches, and packed by hand. We buy from a short list of suppliers we have worked with for years rather than whatever a trade show is offering that season. When a stone is not right, we do not list it.

Photographs are taken of the actual piece that ships. Colour and scale vary between natural objects, and a listing is meant to set expectations rather than promise a specific shade.

Crystals and ritual objects

Some of what we stock is simply beautiful. Some is chosen because people have used objects like it to mark a beginning, an ending, or a slow Tuesday. We are happy with either reason, and we do not pretend a stone will do something it cannot.

Crystal and tarot guidance is offered as reflection and ritual, not medical, legal or financial advice. Take what serves you and leave the rest. If you want to read more, the journal covers the practice in more detail.

Readings

We hold tarot and crystal readings online over video. You choose the format and length that suits you, send a booking request, and we agree on a time by email before anything is charged.

Readings are held as bookings: once confirmed, they can be rescheduled with notice, but they cannot be refunded, because the time was set aside for you.

Orders and returns

Orders ship across India within 2–3 working days, free above ₹1,500. Crystals are natural objects, so slight variation is part of their character. If something arrives damaged or is not as described, tell us within 7 days and we will replace it or refund you in full.
