Good to know.

Shipping

Orders are shipped across India in 2–3 working days from the workshop. Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,500 and above; below that a flat ₹99 is added at checkout. Each piece is wrapped by hand in tissue with a small care note.

Returns & exchanges

Crystals are natural objects, and slight variations are part of their character. If a piece arrives damaged or is not as described, write to us within 7 days of delivery at hello@astrovetro.com and we will happily replace it or refund you in full.

  • Return requests within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items should be returned in their original packaging.
  • Refunds are issued within 5–7 working days of receiving the return.
  • Readings are not refundable once booked and held, but can be rescheduled with notice.

Privacy

Astro Vetro collects only what we need to serve you: your name, email, delivery address and order history. We use this to process orders, confirm readings and — only if you choose — to send occasional journal letters. We never sell your data.

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by writing to hello@astrovetro.com or using the unsubscribe link on an email.

Terms

By placing an order you agree that prices are shown in Indian rupees, payment is due at checkout, and a product becomes yours once it has shipped and been marked delivered. Readings are held as bookings and confirmed by email.

Crystal and tarot guidance is offered as reflection and ritual, not medical, legal or financial advice. Take what serves you, and leave the rest.

FAQ

How long does shipping take?
2–3 working days within India, with free delivery above ₹1,500.

Can I change my order after placing it?
Yes — as long as it has not yet shipped. Write to hello@astrovetro.com with your order number.

How are readings held?
Online readings happen over video. We agree on a time by email after you request a booking, then send the link.

Which payment methods do you accept?
UPI, cards and cash on delivery.
