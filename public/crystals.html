How a piece is chosen

Three questions we ask.

Nothing reaches the collection on trend alone. Each piece has to survive all three.

01

Does it sit still

Weight and base matter more than size. A tower that will not stand up on a shelf is a piece that ends up in a drawer.

02

Does the finish hold

Pieces are turned by hand, so edges are softened and bases are flattened. It should be comfortable to pick up and put down.

03

Is it worth the shelf

Would we keep it ourselves? If a stone only makes sense while it is in the box, it is not one of ours.
