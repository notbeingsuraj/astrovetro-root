The practice

Make space for ritual.

Ritual does not have to be elaborate to be real. It can be a candle lit on the same evening each week, a stone held while you breathe, a question written down before you sleep. The practice matters more than the objects, but the right objects make it easier to begin.

About

Why we started.

AstroVetro began with a simple frustration: most of what is sold as spiritual is loud, and most of what is genuinely useful is not sold at all. We wanted the opposite of that — a small, quiet practice built around objects that are honestly described, made to last, and priced without theatre.

Everything is chosen in person, in small batches, and packed by hand. If a stone is not right, we do not list it.
