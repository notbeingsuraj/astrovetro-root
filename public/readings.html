The ritual experience

How a reading works.

01

You bring a question

Not a neatly worded one. Whatever is genuinely on your mind is enough — the reading meets you where you actually are.

02

We talk, then the cards

We spend the first few minutes on context, then work through the spread. You are welcome to speak, or to simply listen.

03

You leave with language

Not predictions. A clearer way to hold what you already know, and usually one concrete thing to do next.
